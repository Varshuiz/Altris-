01 / STATUTE

Regulatory Commitment

Statutory base under Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018 and related executive decrees.

ALTRIS TRADING FZCO maintains unconditional adherence to UAE Federal Laws and Executive Regulations concerning Anti-Money Laundering, Combating the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT), and Combating Proliferation Financing. The firm operates in strict alignment with Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019, Ministry of Economy Precious Metals Directives, and the DMCC Rules for Risk-Based Due Diligence in the Sourcing of Precious Metals.

Federal Alignment

UAE Cabinet Directives

Formalized compliance under Cabinet Resolution No. 29 of 2021 regarding Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs). Direct registration and mandatory operational reporting via the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit goAML system.

Free Zone Sourcing Mandate

DMCC Responsible Sourcing

Systematic operational alignment with the 5-Step Due Diligence Framework set forth in the OECD Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains of Minerals from Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas (CAHRAs).

Trade transactions are physically settled exclusively inside audited free zone vaults or recognized bonded logistics terminals. ALTRIS executes zero cash-settled trades or off-ledger exchanges under any circumstance.

02 / PROTOCOL

Counterparty Due Diligence

Exhaustive KYC / KYB verification protocols applied across all corporate counterparties.

Counterparty onboarding at ALTRIS TRADING FZCO requires comprehensive legal and operational vetting prior to entering into master physical bullion trading agreements. Onboarding is conducted exclusively for established institutional entities, authorized refineries, and licensed trading desks.

03 / CUSTODY

Record Keeping & Audit Trail

Immutable chronological archiving of physical metal custody transfers and settlement records.

Pursuant to Article (24) of Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2019, all records pertaining to commercial transactions, customs declarations, purity assay certifications, and client due diligence dossiers are preserved for a statutory minimum duration of five (5) full calendar years from the date of transactional execution or the date of counterparty relationship termination.

04 / DISCLOSURE

Transparency & Cooperation

Bilateral reporting relationships with domestic oversight agencies and global supervisory networks.

ALTRIS maintains proactive communication channels with the UAE Ministry of Economy, the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU), DMCC compliance inspection divisions, and international law enforcement authorities. The company enforces internal mechanisms for spontaneous filing of Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) and Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) without tipping off affected counterparties.
